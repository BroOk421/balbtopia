Pixel-art wings (Oct 30 2026). Run from this folder; output goes to cher/, bats/, vamp2/.
  cherub.py   -> Angel Wings, Tiny Angel Wings (cherlib.py: feather drawing)
  bats.py     -> Devil Wings, Tiny Devil Wings (bat style)
  vampire2.py -> Vampire Wings, Tiny Vampire Wings (the owner's sample wing vampire_sample.png, sampled into
                 ref_grid.txt and recoloured: black bones, red skin)
Each makes wings_<name>.png: 6 frames of 240x230 (1 art pixel = 5 body px), copied to www/assets/items/.
